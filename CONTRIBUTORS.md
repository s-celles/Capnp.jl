# Contributors

Thank you to everyone who has contributed to Capnp.jl!

## History & Acknowledgments

This project originated from the initial work and foundations laid by **Ondřej Slámečka**. We are deeply grateful for his early contributions to bringing Cap'n Proto to the Julia ecosystem.

As the package has evolved, its development has taken different architectural directions to support new features (such as experimental RPC and a new trait-based code-generation API). Please note that Ondřej is not the maintainer of this current version. All issues, questions, and feature requests should be directed to the active maintainers of this repository.
